\begin{frame}
\frametitle{AO Policy}
\begin{itemize}
\item
\textbf{ACADEMIC}
\begin{itemize}
\item
Formative AO -- revise at your own time
\item
Summative AO -- revise with a  proctor, e.g.,  in class or during office hours
\item 
End of Term (EOT) AO -- no revision
\end{itemize}
\item
\textbf{HABITS OF WORK}
\begin{itemize}
\item
Sprint or Exit Ticket -- no revision
\item 
Homework -- see \textit{Homework Policy}
\item
Diagnostic AO -- no revision\footnote{This is meant to assess the readiness for EOT and Regents}.
\item
Essays -- revise at your own time
\end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}
\frametitle{Communication Policy}
\pause

\begin{itemize}
\item
Students must check their school email  account \textbf{at least} twice a day. Typically, once before school, and once after school.\pause

\begin{frame}
\frametitle{Homework Policy Addendum}
\pause

In addition to our daily homework draw, we will use Khan Academy as a parallel homework system. These assignments will give you extra practice and an opportunity to make up for a low homework grade.
\vspace{\stretch{1}}

At the end of each marking period, we will compare your daily homework draw grade with your Khan Academy homework grade and record the \textbf{\color{red}higher of the two}.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}
\frametitle{Tools Policy}
\pause

If you need a pencil, please raise your hand and ask for one.
\vspace{\stretch{1}}

Once your request is approved, take a pencil from the container and sign your \textbf{initials} in the pencil register on the wall.
\vspace{\stretch{1}}

When you are finished, return the pencil to the container and check off your \textbf{initials} in the register.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}
\frametitle{Bathroom Policy Addendum}
\pause

Please plan your bathroom trips to minimize your absence during math class. (Whenever possible, use the bathroom at other time.)
\pause
\vspace{\stretch{1}}

If you need to use the bathroom, please wait until \textit{One Mic Time} has ended unless it is an emergency. If you ask to leave during \textit{One Mic Time}, I will remind you of this policy.\pause
\vspace{\stretch{1}}

On your way out to the bathroom or get water, \textbf{sign your initials in the register} and put the trash bin between the frame and the door so that it doesn't lock. On your way in, remove the trash bin and return to your seat quietly.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}
\frametitle{Cleanup Policy}
\pause

There are two trash bins in this room, one at the front and one at the back. Please throw away your trash and clean up any mess promptly.
\pause
\vspace{\stretch{1}}

At the end of each class, return the desks and chairs to their original arrangement.
\pause
\vspace{\stretch{1}}

Return your binder or folder to its designated place.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}
\frametitle{Dismissal Policy}
\large
\pause

\begin{center}
\textit{The bell doesn't dismiss you---I do.}

\hfill--- Confucius, circa 500 BCE (allegedly)

\end{center}
\pause

All students must remain seated until I dismiss the class.
\pause

\textbf{Before dismissal:}
\pause

\begin{enumerate}[label=$\bullet$]
\item All pencils, markers, calculators, laptops, and other supplies must be returned to their proper places.
\pause

\item Class materials must be put back in the binder, homework must be put away in the backpack, and all personal belongings must be removed from desks and the floor.
\pause

\item All trash must be thrown away, desks and chairs must be returned to their original arrangement, and the classroom must be left clean.
\end{enumerate}
